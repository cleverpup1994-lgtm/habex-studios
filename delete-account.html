Fastest way: in the game

Open Habex Quest → tap ⚙️ (top right) → Delete account → Delete forever.

If the game asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign back in, and tap Delete account once more.

Can't open the game?

Email abelardo599@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Habex Quest account", from the email you use for the game, and include your runner name. We will delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in, runner name and profile
  • Your hero, island, coins, gems, gear, trophies and progress, including your cloud save
  • Your friends list, friend requests and private messages you sent
  • Your clan membership (if you lead a clan, it passes to another member)

What may be kept

Reports about abuse may be kept up to 90 days for safety. Likes you gave other islands stay as a count without your name.

Eliminar tu cuenta (Español)

Abre Habex Quest → toca ⚙️ (arriba a la derecha) → Eliminar cuenta → Eliminar para siempre.

Si el juego te pide entrar de nuevo, cierra sesión, vuelve a entrar y toca Eliminar cuenta otra vez.

¿No puedes abrir el juego? Escribe a abelardo599@gmail.com con el asunto "Eliminar mi cuenta de Habex Quest", desde el correo de tu cuenta y con tu nombre de corredor. La eliminaremos en un plazo de 30 días.

Se borra: tu cuenta, nombre, perfil, héroe, isla, monedas, gemas, equipo, progreso (incluida la copia en la nube), amigos, solicitudes y los mensajes privados que enviaste. Los reportes de abuso pueden guardarse hasta 90 días.
